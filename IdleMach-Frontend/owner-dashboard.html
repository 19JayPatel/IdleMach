Owner workflow reference

Business rules
  • Only approved machines appear in the marketplace.
  • Only active machines can receive booking requests.
  • Available Capacity = Total Capacity − Booked Capacity.
  • Owner acceptance precedes buyer payment; payment precedes booking confirmation.
  • Completion requires successful payment, a confirmed or in-progress booking, and finished work.
  • Reviews are permitted only after a booking is completed.
These rules describe the documented IdleMach workflow. This prototype does not enforce them on a server. Reference only
