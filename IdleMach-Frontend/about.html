About IdleMach

A Marketplace for Manufacturing Capacity.

IdleMach connects machine owners who have unused manufacturing capacity with buyers who need manufacturing work done. Owners list available machine hours; buyers discover suitable capacity and request work through one structured booking workflow.

Turn Idle Capacity Into Opportunity.

Explore Machines List Your Machine

Frontend prototype: accounts, live listings, bookings, payments, and reviews are not connected.

Marketplace, not machine seller.

  • IdleMach does not manufacture or sell machines.
  • Listings describe existing manufacturing capacity and its availability.
  • Machine owners decide which booking requests to accept.

The platform is designed around the documented order of listing, request, owner acceptance, payment, work, and review.

Our purpose

Unused machine hours are still capacity.

Manufacturing machines can have hours that are not producing work. IdleMach gives owners a way to present that capacity and gives buyers a way to find it.

A listing records the machine, process, owner, location, rate, capacity, and availability. Buyers can compare this information before requesting work instead of starting from an informal search.

Who uses IdleMach

Three roles, one marketplace.

Each role has a clear part in the workflow, from listing capacity to reviewing platform activity.

Machine Owners

Businesses with existing machines and available hours.

  • List unused manufacturing capacity.
  • Manage machine details and availability.
  • Receive and respond to booking requests.

Buyers

Businesses that need manufacturing work done.

  • Discover manufacturing machines.
  • Compare relevant specifications and availability.
  • Request manufacturing work.

Admins

Platform operators who review and monitor activity.

  • Review machine listings.
  • Manage users.
  • Monitor bookings, payments, and platform activity.

Admin review is a listing and platform check. It is not a certification or an inspection of machine condition.

How the marketplace works

A clear sequence from listing to review.

Every booking follows the same documented path. Availability is checked before a request, the owner decides before payment, and successful payment confirms the booking.

Our principles

A practical approach to marketplace clarity.

These are intended platform behaviours based on the documented workflow. They are not certifications or performance guarantees.

Clear machine information

Listings are structured to show the machine, process, owner, location, rate, capacity, and availability before a request is sent.

Availability awareness

Buyers check availability before requesting capacity, and owners recheck it before accepting a request.

Owner-controlled booking decisions

Owners decide which booking requests to accept. No request moves to payment without that decision.

Structured booking workflow

Request, acceptance, payment, confirmation, work, completion, and review stay in the documented order.

Responsible platform administration

Admins review listings and monitor platform activity according to their permissions.
