03 · Spacing and layout

Spacing scale and containers

A 4px-based spacing scale keeps rhythm consistent. Containers cap content at 1180px and expand their side padding on larger screens.

Spacing tokens

--space-1 4px
--space-2 8px
--space-3 12px
--space-4 16px
--space-5 24px
--space-6 32px
--space-7 48px
--space-8 64px

Radii, borders, shadows

  • --radius-control 6px for buttons, inputs, and alerts.
  • --radius-card 10px for cards, panels, and the preview toast.
  • Borders use --color-border; interactive borders use --color-border-strong.
  • --shadow-card for resting surfaces; --shadow-raised for overlays only.
  • Grid relies on Bootstrap 5 rows and columns, not custom layout frameworks.

Container preview

This panel sits inside .container-im. At 1180px and above it stops growing; below that it keeps 16px side padding, rising to 24px from 576px.
